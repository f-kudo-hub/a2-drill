Subscription

Premium is a monthly auto-renewing subscription with a 1-week free trial for new subscribers. It renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can cancel in iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions. To use Premium on a new iPhone, tap "Restore purchases" in the App. Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com).

About the questions

All questions are original and written for practice at the A2 level described for the JFT-Basic test. The App is unofficial and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Japan Foundation. It does not contain official test questions, and scores in the App are not official scores.
