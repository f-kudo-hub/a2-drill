Subscription

Premium is a monthly auto-renewing subscription with a 1-week free trial for new subscribers. It renews automatically unless cancelled before the end of the current period (on iPhone, at least 24 hours before). On iPhone, cancel in Settings > your name > Subscriptions. On Android, cancel in the Play Store > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions (open in Google Play). To use Premium on a new phone, sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account and tap "Restore purchases" in the App. Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play (refund requests).
