Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-09

A2 Drill (the "App") does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. Your answers, scores and language setting are saved only on your device and are deleted when you delete the App.

The App has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The only network communication is with the store you installed the App from — Apple's App Store on iPhone, or Google Play on Android — when you buy or restore the subscription. Apple or Google processes that payment under its own privacy policy (Apple, Google); we never receive your payment details.

Listening questions are read aloud by your device's built-in Japanese voice. No audio is recorded.

The free web practice test on this site also stores nothing and uses no cookies or analytics.
